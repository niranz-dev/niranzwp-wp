name: Alt text
description: How to write alt text for images on this site. Load before setting alt on any image.
---

Alt text describes what an image shows to someone who cannot see it. It is not
a caption, not a credit line, and not a place for keywords.

## Rules

- Never begin with "image of", "photo of", "picture of". A screen reader has
  already announced that it is an image.
- Under 125 characters. Longer than that and most screen readers cut it off.
- Name people when they are recognisable and named in the article.
- Say what is happening, not what is in frame. "A welder rests on a girder
  above the harbour" beats "man, girder, harbour, crane".
- Photographer and agency credit belong in the caption, never here.
- Leave alt empty for images that carry no information -- dividers, spacers,
  decorative flourishes. An empty alt tells a screen reader to skip it, which
  is correct. Omitting the attribute entirely is not the same thing.

## Do not

- Do not stuff keywords. It does not help ranking and it makes the page worse
  for the people alt text exists for.
- Do not repeat the caption verbatim. The reader gets both.
